
Redis usage guide: [Redis docs](https://redis.io/docs/latest/)

This summer, I interned at ByteDance in Silicon Valley, where I worked on a distributed key-value database. A lot of what we built was based on Redis, which helped me understand Redis as much more than just a cache.

I like Redis's elegant single-threaded model, its use of copy-on-write, and the way those design choices make efficient use of memory. The design is simple on the surface but highly optimized underneath, and that combination is what I find most interesting.
